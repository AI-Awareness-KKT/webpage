Movie Recommender Project

What Does It Do?

Loved a movie and want something like it? Type its name, choose how many suggestions you want, and the app lists movies with similar stories, genres, directors and actors.

It searches the IMDb Top 1000 movies and gives every suggestion a similarity score from 0 to 1. The higher the score, the more the two movies have in common.

How Does a Computer “Read” a Movie?

A computer cannot watch a film, so we describe each movie with words: its story summary (overview), its genre, its director and its four main actors.

  • TF-IDF: This method turns those words into numbers. Words that are special to one movie (like “dream” or “heist”) get a high score, while common words (like “the” or “man”) get a low score.
  • Feature vector: Each movie becomes a long list of these scores — its “fingerprint”.

How Does It Find Similar Movies?

The app compares the fingerprint of your movie with every other movie using cosine similarity. Think of each fingerprint as an arrow: if two arrows point in nearly the same direction, the movies are alike.

  • Score close to 1: very similar (shared genre, director, actors or story words).
  • Score close to 0: almost nothing in common.
  • If two movies tie, the one with the higher IMDb rating comes first.

The score measures shared content — it does not predict whether you will enjoy the movie.

How Does It All Work Together?

Here’s what happens step by step:

  • You type a movie name (the box suggests titles as you type).
  • You choose how many recommendations you want (1 to 999).
  • The app finds your movie in its catalog of 1,000 films.
  • It calculates the similarity score between your movie and every other movie.
  • It shows the top matches with their year, genre, IMDb rating and score.

Features of the Project

  • Content-based recommendations from 7 features per movie.
  • Title suggestions for misspelled or unknown names.
  • Asks you to pick the right version when two movies share a name.
  • Runs fully offline on the computer — no internet or API key needed.
